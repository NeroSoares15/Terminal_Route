# Terminal Route

## Short Description
A PS1-style horror bus-driving demo where every stop feels wrong.

## Tagline
Keep the bus on the road. Reach the stops. Do not stare too long.

## Page Description
Terminal Route is a short playable horror driving demo made for a university prototype deadline. You drive a night bus through a looping rural route where the passengers, the road, and the mirror slowly stop behaving like they should.

Steer carefully, pull close to the stops, watch the opposite lane, and use the mirror only when you have to. Missing too many stops, losing control, crashing, or staring at the wrong passenger for too long can end the route early.

This is a demo build, so the focus is the core loop, atmosphere, and proof of concept rather than a finished full game.

## Controls
- A / D or Left / Right Arrow: steer
- F: look back / mirror view
- Enter or mouse click: menu confirm

## Demo Features
- 8-stop night bus route
- Stop accuracy and missed-stop failure
- Oncoming bus hazard
- Mirror scares and sanity pressure
- PS1-inspired visuals, low-res rendering, fog, and CRT-style UI
- Win and loss result screens
