# Terminal Route

## Short Description
A PS1-style horror bus-driving game about reaching the last stop without trusting the road, the passengers, or the mirror.

## Tagline
The last route is not on the schedule.

## Page Description
Terminal Route is a first-person horror driving game made as a final university prototype. You drive the last night bus across a route that should be familiar, but every stop pulls the world further out of place.

Keep the bus steady, pull close enough to each stop, control the doors, count the passengers, and check the mirror only when the route gives you a reason to. The road is narrow, the opposite lane is not empty, and the people behind you may not be sitting where they were a moment ago.

The final build expands the original demo with a proper intro, real audio, a denser roadside environment, a sanity system, passenger-counting pressure, door management, a tutorial flow, and an unlockable Nightmare Mode with 20 faster, more dangerous stops.

This is still a student prototype rather than a commercial full release, but it is built to show the complete core loop, atmosphere, presentation style, and future potential of the game.

## Controls
- A / D or Left / Right Arrow: steer
- F: look back / mirror view
- E: open / close doors
- Q: confirm passenger count while checking the mirror
- Enter, Space, or mouse click: menu confirm / skip intro
- Esc: pause / back
- F11 or menu button: fullscreen

## Final Build Features
- Story intro with skippable voice-over and eye-opening transition into gameplay
- Tutorial flow for the main mechanics
- 8-stop normal route with stop accuracy and missed-stop failure
- Unlockable Nightmare Mode with 20 stops, faster pacing, and stacked hazards
- Door management: leaving with doors open puts passengers at risk
- Passenger counting after stops
- Sanity pressure shown through the bus dashboard
- Mirror scares, close passenger threats, and reasons to look back
- Oncoming bus hazards and loose objects on the road
- Denser PS1-style environment with buildings, parked cars, bus stops, trees, fog, and road props
- Low-resolution rendering, CRT/VHS visual treatment, and diegetic green terminal UI
- Win, loss, credits, controls, pause, and result screens
